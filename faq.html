Frequently Asked Questions

What is Temp Mail Pro?

Temp Mail Pro provides temporary email addresses that can be used to receive messages without using your personal email.

How do I create a temporary email?

Click the "Generate Email" button on the home page. A temporary email address will be created for you.

How long does an email address last?

The current website provides a temporary mailbox with a countdown timer. When the timer expires, you can generate a new email address.

Can I receive messages?

Yes. Incoming messages appear automatically in the inbox while the temporary email address is active.

Can I send emails?

No. Temp Mail Pro is designed for receiving messages only. You cannot send outgoing emails from a temporary inbox.

Can I delete messages?

Yes. You can delete individual messages from your inbox when you no longer need them.

Do I need my personal email?

No. The purpose of the service is to provide a temporary email address without requiring your personal email address.

Are temporary email addresses private?

Temporary email addresses can help you avoid sharing your personal email address. However, you should not treat a temporary inbox as a secure place for sensitive or confidential information.

What happens when the timer expires?

When the temporary mailbox expires, the address and its messages may no longer be available. You can generate a new temporary email address when needed.

Can I use temporary email for banking or important accounts?

No. Temporary email is not recommended for banking, financial services, password recovery, work accounts, or other important services that require long-term access.

Can I use temporary email for everything?

Temporary email is best for short-term and low-risk uses, such as testing websites, receiving verification messages, and keeping your personal inbox free from unwanted messages.

Are messages stored permanently?

No. Messages received through a temporary mailbox are intended for temporary use and may be deleted when the mailbox expires or when you delete them.
