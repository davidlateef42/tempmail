Frequently Asked Questions

What is Temp Mail Pro?

Temp Mail Pro provides temporary email addresses that can be used to receive messages without using your personal email.

How do I create a temporary email?

Click the "Generate Email" button on the home page. A temporary email address will be created for you.

How long does an email address last?

The current website provides a temporary mailbox with a countdown timer. When the timer expires, you can generate a new email address.

Can I receive messages?

Yes. Incoming messages appear automatically in the inbox while the temporary email address is active.

Can I delete messages?

Yes. You can delete individual messages from your inbox.

Do I need my personal email?

No. The purpose of the service is to provide a temporary email address without requiring your personal email address.

Can I use temporary email for everything?

Temporary email is best for short-term and low-risk uses. Important accounts may require a permanent email address.
