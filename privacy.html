Privacy Policy

Welcome to Temp Mail Pro. This Privacy Policy explains how Temp Mail Pro handles information when you use our temporary email service.

Temporary Email Addresses

Temp Mail Pro provides temporary email addresses for receiving messages. These addresses are intended for temporary use and may expire automatically.

Messages

Messages received through a temporary email address may be stored temporarily so that they can be displayed in your inbox. Messages may be deleted when the temporary mailbox expires or when you choose to delete them.

Personal Information

You do not need to provide your personal email address to generate a temporary email address.

Third-Party Services

Temp Mail Pro uses external services to provide email delivery and mailbox functionality. Information necessary to operate the service may be processed by those services.

Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect information processed through the service. However, no internet service can guarantee complete security.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the contact page.
