Privacy Policy

Welcome to Temp Mail Pro. This Privacy Policy explains how Temp Mail Pro handles information when you use our temporary email service.

Temporary Email Addresses

Temp Mail Pro provides temporary email addresses for receiving messages. These addresses are designed for short-term use and may expire automatically after the temporary mailbox period ends.

Messages and Mailbox Data

Messages received through a temporary email address may be processed and stored temporarily so they can be displayed in your inbox. Messages may be deleted when you delete them or when the temporary mailbox expires.

Because temporary mailboxes are designed for short-term use, you should not use Temp Mail Pro to store important, permanent, or highly sensitive information.

Personal Information

You do not need to provide your personal email address, password, or other personal information to generate a temporary email address through Temp Mail Pro.

Information Needed to Operate the Service

Information related to a temporary mailbox may be processed when necessary to create the mailbox, deliver incoming messages, display messages in the inbox, refresh mailbox status, or delete messages.

Third-Party Services

Temp Mail Pro relies on external services and infrastructure to provide email delivery and mailbox functionality. Information necessary to operate these features may be processed by those service providers.

These third-party services may have their own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing the policies of any external service when appropriate.

Cookies and Local Storage

Temp Mail Pro may use browser storage technologies, such as local storage, to support website features such as remembering certain inbox information and message read status.

These technologies are used to help the website function and are not intended to collect your personal email password or other sensitive account credentials.

Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect information processed through the service. However, no website, internet connection, or online service can guarantee complete security.

For this reason, you should avoid sending or storing sensitive information through a temporary email address.

Children and Minors

Temp Mail Pro is intended for general internet use. Users should follow the laws and age requirements that apply to them in their location.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our contact page.
